# SO-101 MuJoCo model (VARA copy)

This folder started as a copy of `Simulation/SO101/` from
[TheRobotStudio/SO-ARM100](https://github.com/TheRobotStudio/SO-ARM100): an MJCF generated from the Onshape
CAD with the [onshape-to-robot](https://github.com/Rhoban/onshape-to-robot) plugin, in the "new calibration"
convention (every joint's virtual zero is the middle of its range, the one LeRobot's calibration produces),
with STS3215 motor parameters adapted from the Open Duck Mini project. Upstream also ships an "old
calibration" variant, a wrist-camera variant, a leader-arm URDF and the leader/camera meshes; none of that
is kept here (see NOTES). Upstream's gripper note still applies: LeRobot reports the gripper on a 0 (closed)
to 100 (open) scale, the MJCF has a hinge joint in radians; our mapping lives in `arm/driver.py` (real arm)
and `arm/sim_robot.py` (simulation).

Files:

- `so101_new_calib.xml`: the robot (edited, see NOTES). Included by the two files below; never load it alone.
- `arm_only.xml`: robot + floor + lights + cameras. Used by `arm/ik.py` and `rl/handoff_env.py`.
- `scene_ours.xml`: robot + table + tray + objects + obstacles + hand box. GENERATED by `build_scene.py`.
- `build_scene.py`: `python -m sim.model.build_scene` renders `scene_ours.xml` from `config/objects.yaml`,
  `config/scene.yaml` and `config/fake_scene.json`; its docstring lists every body and geom name and the
  pick geometry that `arm/sim_robot.py` relies on.
- `fit_gripper.py`: `python -m sim.model.fit_gripper` prints the `tip`/`grasp` sites and the gripper
  collision boxes from the mesh vertices (re-run only if the meshes change).
- `assets/`: the 13 STL meshes the robot file references.
- Viewer: `python -m mujoco.viewer --mjcf=sim/model/scene_ours.xml` (from the repo root).

## NOTES (VARA edits, HackHarvard 2026)

- **Frame.** `so101_new_calib.xml`: the root body is `<body name="base" pos="-0.0388353 0 0">`, so the
  shoulder_pan axis passes through x = y = 0 and MuJoCo world coordinates are our table frame (origin under
  the pan axis, +x out from the arm at pan 0, +y to the arm's left, +z up, table at z = 0). Positive pan
  turns the arm toward -y; positive shoulder_lift / elbow_flex / wrist_flex pitch the links down (measured
  in `tests/test_ik.py`). The three pitch joints share one axis (+y at pan 0), so the arm is a planar chain:
  shoulder_lift axis at (x 0.0304, z 0.1166), upper arm 0.116 m, forearm 0.135 m, wrist_flex axis to `tip`
  0.1638 m in the plane (the link plane is offset 1.83 cm to -y, the tip is back on the center line).
- **Sites** in body `gripper`, both with x axis out of the jaws (`pitch = asin(a_z)`) and z axis along the
  closing direction toward the moving jaw:
  - `tip` at `(-0.0068, -0.0002, -0.1026)`: the midpoint of the fixed-jaw tip and the moving-jaw tip with the
    `gripper` joint at its closed limit, measured from the mesh vertices by `fit_gripper.py` (the brief's
    -0.0069 -0.0005 -0.1033 was within 1 mm). This is the point `arm.ik.fk` reports, `arm.replay.validate`
    checks and A6 tape-measures.
  - `grasp` at `(0.0178, -0.0002, -0.1026)`: the grasp center, 2.5 cm from the fixed finger box's inner face
    at the tip level (`fit_gripper.GRASP_FROM_FIXED_FACE`), 2.46 cm from `tip` along the closing direction.
    The SO-101 jaw is asymmetric: only the moving jaw swings and `tip` is 0.3 mm from the fixed finger's
    inner face, so an object centered on `tip` is inside that finger. A pick therefore puts `grasp` on the
    object's center line (`arm.ik.solve_grasp`, `arm.ik.fk_grasp`); the 4 cm objects then keep 5 mm (the IK
    position tolerance) from the fixed finger while the fully open gripper (1.0 rad) descends, and the real
    jaws push the object 5 to 7.5 mm toward the fixed jaw when they close.
- **Gripper collisions.** The convex hulls of `wrist_roll_follower_so101_v1` and `moving_jaw_so101_v1` would
  fill the space between the open fingers, so those two collision meshes are boxes fitted to the mesh
  vertices: `gripper_housing` (AABB of the fixed part above the finger root, 6.5 x 5.2 x 4.1 cm),
  `finger_fixed` (one box per finger, inner face on the mesh's inner face through its tip and root extremes,
  thickness and width the mean of the tip and root cross sections: 1.6 x 2.0 x 6.7 cm) and `finger_moving`
  (1.3 x 1.7 x 5.9 cm). The fingers are wedges, so the boxes are up to ~1 cm too fat on the outer side near
  the tips and up to 0.7 cm too thin on the outer side at the roots; the inner faces and the tips are within
  2 mm (checked by `tests/test_model.py`). Jaw gap between the inner faces at the tip level: 0.2 cm closed
  (-0.17 rad), 1.6 cm at 0, 3.8 cm at 0.3, 5.9 cm at 0.6 rad. The moving finger's tip rises as the jaw opens
  (0.4 cm above the fixed tip level at 0.6 rad, 2.4 cm at 1.0 rad) and the gap narrows toward the hinge
  (0.6 rad: 5.0 cm at 3 cm above the tips, 4.0 cm at 5 cm; 1.0 rad: 8.8 cm at 2.5 cm, 8.1 cm at 3 cm, 5.6 cm
  at 5 cm, 4.7 cm at 6 cm); the housing closes it about 7 cm above the tips, so an object picked at
  mid-height may not extend more than ~6 cm above the tip level (ours: at most 5.25 cm).
- **Collision classes.** `class="collision"` geoms have `contype="1" conaffinity="1"`. Scene bodies use
  `contype="1" conaffinity="3"`, so a held object switched to `contype="2" conaffinity="2"` stops colliding
  with the robot but still hits everything else. Robot self-collisions are simulated (parent-child pairs
  excluded by MuJoCo); the brief says SimRobot does not score them.
- **`arm_only.xml`**: the robot, a floor plane at z = 0, lights, an `overhead` camera where
  `config/scene.yaml` puts the real one and a `side` camera; `timestep 0.002`, `implicitfast`.
- **`scene_ours.xml`** is generated; `tests/test_model.py` compares it with a fresh render and fails on any
  difference in names, shapes, sizes, masses, collision classes, mocap bodies or tray walls (A11 changing
  `objects.yaml`, tray changes in `scene.yaml`); a difference only in initial poses or the camera (A15 moving
  the cereal box in `fake_scene.json`, B4 measuring the camera) is cosmetic because `SimRobot.reset` writes
  every pose, so it only warns. Re-run `python -m sim.model.build_scene` either way.
- **Removed from the upstream copy**: `so101_old_calib.xml`, `so101_new_calib_camera.xml`, the upstream
  `scene.xml` and `joints_properties.xml` (already inlined in the robot file), and the leader/camera meshes
  `Handle_SO101.stl`, `Trigger_SO101.stl`, `Wrist_Roll_SO101.stl`, `wrist_camera_mount_so101_v1.stl`,
  `wrist_camera_so101_v1.stl`.
